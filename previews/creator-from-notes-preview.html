Movement vs lag — try this

Tap a Jump button below. Movement: the button presses instantly, the note changes instantly, and the section slides open smoothly in 0.35 seconds — and you can tap another section right away, while it is still moving. Lag would be: tap, nothing happens, then a late, stuttering jump, and taps in between get ignored. If it feels instant + smooth, that is movement. If it feels delayed + jumpy, that is lag.

What each type means — layman's rubric

Type 1 — Planetary: Controls all the energy of one planet. Can tame weather, volcanoes, and earthquakes, and power everything from the planet itself. Can't yet harness a whole star.

Type 2 — Stellar: Controls all the energy of one star. Can build a Dyson swarm around its sun, settle a whole solar system, and travel easily between planets. Can't yet use other stars at scale.

Type 3 — Galactic: Controls all the energy of one galaxy. Can travel between stars, use billions of stars at once, and move planets. Can't yet leave its galaxy or control another one.

All skills are earned: No type hands a character everything at once. No matter how advanced the civilization is, skills are earned in the story.

Type 4 — Universal: Controls all the energy of one universe. Can cross the universe, bend space and time, and use whole galaxies like tools. Can't yet leave its universe.

Type 5 — Multiversal: Controls energy across many universes. Can step between realities and use more than one universe at once. Its only real limit is what you decide it is.

Beyond — You define it: No preset power level. You name what this civilization commands, and the gear check measures against your own description instead of a fixed scale.
